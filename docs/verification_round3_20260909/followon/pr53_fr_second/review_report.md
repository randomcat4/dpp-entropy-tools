## RPF primary-source check

The theorem uses the RPF theorem in the exact finite-alphabet full-shift case. Stoyanov's RPF statement assumes a one-sided shift of finite type with an aperiodic transition matrix and a Hölder potential on a Hölder space, and gives a simple maximal positive eigenvalue plus a spectral gap for the transfer operator. See Luchezar Stoyanov, “On Gibbs measures and spectra of Ruelle transfer operators,” arXiv:1703.04276, especially the setup and theorem statement at https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.04276.

For analytic dependence, Cioletti and Silva record analytic dependence of the Ruelle operator on Hölder spaces and pressure analyticity under the spectral-gap framework. See Leandro Cioletti and Eduardo A. Silva, “Spectral Properties of the Ruelle Operator on the Walters Class over Compact Spaces,” arXiv:1511.01579 / Nonlinearity, at https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.01579.

Theorem FR's use is narrower than both sources: the alphabet is `{0,1}`, the full-shift transition matrix is aperiodic, `G_s` is normalized so the eigenvalue stays exactly `1`, strict positivity holds for real small `s`, and the fixed Hölder norm comes from Section 5's exponential tail estimate.

## Attack notes and preserved limitations

- No finite-window derivative extrapolation is used. The matching KL lower bound is first passed to the entropy-rate inequality, and only then is the analytic rate formula differentiated.
- Rare complete configurations are kept. Lemma 4.1 controls `M_x^{-1}` uniformly in the complete word `x`, without a lower probability bound for that word.
- The complex inverse/conditional argument uses a fixed Hölder exponent and a uniform Neumann radius; it is not a window-dependent compactness argument.
- The RPF step requires strict positivity for real small parameters and a holomorphic perturbation inside one Hölder space. Those hypotheses are supplied by Sections 5-6 after shrinking the interval if necessary.
- The theorem is local near `t=0`. It does not settle the whole legal interval, the later exponential extension, arbitrary measurable symbols, or any positive entropy-rate Jensen gap.

No precise blocker was found. Theorem FR is merge-eligible as a scoped local entropy-rate theorem, with the nonblocking recommendation that `sources.md` add an RPF/g-measure citation if the source ledger is intended to list every external theorem used.
