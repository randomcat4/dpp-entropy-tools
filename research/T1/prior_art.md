# Prior-art status

Novelty is UNCONFIRMED. The entropy Hessian identity, Fisher term, determinant
conditioning and phase-gauge invariance are existing tools, not new claims.

- Russell Lyons, [Determinantal Probability: Basic Properties and Conjectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2707), 2014: baseline finite determinantal
  laws and broader questions. This source is not evidence that the restricted
  bridge lemma is new or that a global entropy conjecture is solved.
- Hideitsu Hino and Keisuke Yano, [An embedding structure of determinantal point process](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41884-024-00156-x)
  ([arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11024)): DPP embedding into log-linear
  exponential families and information-geometric curvature. These geometric
  curvature objects must not be confused with the second derivative of Shannon
  entropy on a marginal-kernel affine path.
- Frank Nielsen, [An Elementary Introduction to Information Geometry](https://franknielsen.github.io/entropy-22-01100-v2.pdf): background on Fisher geometry
  and dual affine structures. A Hessian relation in probability/mixture
  coordinates does not eliminate acceleration terms in nonlinear K coordinates.

The independent audit in audits/prior_art_conditions.md records precise
conditions and source checks. Until a broader equivalence/prior-art search is
complete, the value claim is an event-free implementation of a restricted
structural lemma, suitable as a reusable auxiliary tool, not a novel paper core.
