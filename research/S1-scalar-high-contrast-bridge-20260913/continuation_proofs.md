# Post-PR continuation: Fisher density and an all-contrast sparse wedge

Status: PROVED AS AUTHOR MATHEMATICS / PENDING INDEPENDENT REVIEW. This is not an entropy-rate counterexample, not a proof of universal high-contrast concavity, and not a novelty claim. The assertions below are independent of the historical screen counts.

Fix a measurable E in the circle, rho=|E|, Q_n=T_n(1_E), K_n(a)=aI+cQ_n. All complete words are retained. The parameter is a with c,E fixed. Write V_n=Tr Q_n(I-Q_n). The fixed-process note proves V_n/n->0, and for J frequency intervals V_n<=(2J^2/pi^2)(harmonic(n)+1).

## Theorem F1: a full-law count Fisher floor

In 0<a<a+c<1 let p_x(a) be the complete configuration law and

    F_n(a)=sum_x (p'_x)^2/p_x,
    N_n(x)=|x|,
    v(a,c,rho)=(1-rho)a(1-a)+rho(a+c)(1-a-c).

Then, at every n,

    F_n(a)/n >= 1/[v(a,c,rho)+c^2 V_n/n].                (F1)

Proof. Finite complete probabilities are strictly positive and differentiable. For the complete-law score U=p'/p, E U=0 and

    E[U(N_n-E N_n)]=d_a E N_n=n.

This follows by differentiating E N_n=Tr K_n=n(a+c rho), not by discarding same-cardinality configurations. The determinantal count variance is

    Var(N_n)=Tr K_n(I-K_n)=n v+c^2 V_n.

Cauchy-Schwarz proves F_n Var(N_n)>=n^2. No lower bound on an individual atom, full/empty event, or conditionally rare history occurs in (F1).

## Theorem F2: exact centered Fisher information density

Let a_0=(1-c)/2, 0<c<1, and v_0=(1-c^2)/4. Then

    1/[v_0+c^2 V_n/n] <= F_n(a_0)/n <= 1/v_0.           (F2)

Consequently, for EVERY fixed measurable E,

    lim_(n->infinity) F_n(a_0)/n = 4/(1-c^2).            (F3)

No Fourier regularity, entropy derivative limit, Fisher nonsingularity of the full matrix parameter, or identification modulo a matrix gauge is assumed.

Proof of the upper bound. Diagonalize Q_n once, independently of a. A finite DPP can be generated by choosing its eigenmodes independently, with probabilities lambda_j(a)=a+c lambda_j(Q_n), and then sampling the projection DPP on the chosen eigenvectors. The latter conditional sampling law does not depend on a. This mixture identity follows from expansion of the determinantal generating polynomial, so it concerns the entire occupation distribution rather than just the number of particles.

The output score equals the conditional expectation of the latent independent-Bernoulli score. Jensen's inequality therefore gives

    F_n(a) <= sum_j 1/[lambda_j(a)(1-lambda_j(a))].

At a_0,

    lambda_j(a_0)(1-lambda_j(a_0))
      =v_0+c^2 lambda_j(Q_n)(1-lambda_j(Q_n)) >= v_0.

The upper bound in (F2) follows. Its lower bound is (F1); V_n/n->0 gives (F3).

Explicit finite-size error:

    0 <= 1/v_0-F_n(a_0)/n
      <= (c^2 V_n/n)/[v_0(v_0+c^2 V_n/n)]
      <= c^2 V_n/(n v_0^2).                              (F4)

Thus convergence is uniform on any c range bounded away from 1, for each fixed E, and has an explicit J-band estimate. It is NOT asserted uniform as c->1 without the displayed dependence.

There is also an asymptotic count sufficiency statement in L2. If U is the full-word score at a_0, then

    E[(U-(N_n-E N_n)/v_0)^2] <= c^2 V_n/v_0^2.           (F5)

Indeed expansion of the square gives F_n-n/v_0+c^2 V_n/v_0^2, and (F2) bounds it. The optimal count projection has coefficient n/Var(N_n) and residual F_n-n^2/Var(N_n)>=0. The checker verifies this latter identity from every full atom, not just from traces.

For the normalized bias t=a/(1-c), Fisher information is multiplied by (1-c)^2. Therefore the centered t-information density is 4(1-c)/(1+c), which vanishes as c->1. The diverging a-information in (F3) is not parameterization-independent.

### Explicit approach-to-limit scale

If c^2 V_n/n<=v_0, then F_n/n>=1/(2v_0). Using pi>=3 and harmonic(n)<=1+log n, it suffices that

    [2J^2 c^2/(9n)](log n+2) <= v_0.                    (F6)

The left side decreases for n>=1. The literal certificate checks (F6) at six dyadic n0 values and thereby certifies it for every n>=n0. These are sufficient analytic block lengths, NOT measurements of minimal block lengths and NOT full configuration computations at those large n.

For c=9999/10000, the certified n0 is 65,536 for J=1 and 2,097,152 for J=4. This explains why n<=12 screens can remain far from the centered Fisher-density regime; it does not invalidate their correctly scoped finite results.

## Theorem D: a continuum high-contrast/density exclusion

Let epsilon=1-c in (0,1), a=epsilon/2, d=2epsilon/5, and rho_*=min(rho,1-rho). Define the TRUE fixed-process Shannon entropy-rate chord

    J_E=h_E(a,c)-[h_E(a-d,c)+h_E(a+d,c)]/2.

For every measurable E, if

    rho_* <= 9 epsilon/[100 c log(10/(3epsilon))],       (D1)

then

    J_E > 9 epsilon/100.                               (D2)

In particular, this covers a continuum of c arbitrarily close to 1, arbitrary measurable shapes and arbitrary numbers of frequency bands. It is a theorem about this specified chord, not about every a-chord or h_aa.

### Complete-channel entropy sandwich

Let X be the stationary projection DPP with symbol 1_E. Apply independent channels Y_i|X_i~Ber(a+cX_i). For every finite A,

    E product_(i in A)(a+cX_i)
      =sum_(B subset A) a^(|A|-|B|) c^|B| det((Q_n)_B)
      =det((aI+cQ_n)_A).

Hence the output is exactly the target DPP. Conditional entropy and the one-site upper bound give

    (1-rho)b(a)+rho b(a+c) <= h_E(a,c) <= b(a+c rho).

At a=epsilon/2 the lower bound is b(epsilon/2). By particle-hole symmetry the Jensen chord is unchanged when E is replaced by E^c and its endpoints are exchanged, so assume rho<=1/2. Therefore

    J_E >= G_epsilon(rho)
      :=b(epsilon/2)
        -[b(epsilon/10+c rho)+b(9epsilon/10+c rho)]/2.    (D3)

### Uniform lower estimate

Put phi(t)=-t log t and u(x)=-(1-x)log(1-x). Since u is concave and the linear-in-t term -epsilon t log epsilon cancels in a midpoint gap,

    b(epsilon/2)-[b(epsilon/10)+b(9epsilon/10)]/2
      >= epsilon A,
    A=(log 2)/2+(log(1/10))/20+9log(9/10)/20
      =0.184032103584248... >9/50.                      (D4)

The decimal is explanatory only. The strict rational inequality A>9/50 is proved by the directed log series in the public checker; it can also be checked by the positive atanh series with its geometric remainder.

Concavity of b gives b(x+s)-b(x)<=s b'(x). Averaging at x=epsilon/10 and x=9epsilon/10 yields a slope

    L_epsilon=(1/2)log[((1-epsilon/10)(1-9epsilon/10))
                              /((9/100)epsilon^2)]
              <=log(10/(3epsilon)).

All endpoints remain legal. Thus (D3)-(D4) give

    J_E >= epsilon A-c rho log(10/(3epsilon)).           (D5)

Condition (D1) and A>9/50 imply (D2). This is an all-epsilon proof, not a fit or a finite c grid.

### Sharp scale of this particular sufficient bound

For fixed lambda>=0 and rho=lambda epsilon/log(1/epsilon),

    lim_(epsilon->0) G_epsilon(rho)/epsilon=A-lambda.   (D6)

To see this, the unshifted Bernoulli gap divided by epsilon tends to A. The shift s=c rho is O(epsilon/log(1/epsilon)); expanding each b(epsilon t+s)-b(epsilon t) by its integral derivative gives s log(1/epsilon)+O(s+s^2/epsilon), uniformly for t in {1/10,9/10}. Dividing by epsilon gives lambda+o(1).

Thus every lambda<A eventually yields a true-rate positive chord through this method. For lambda>A the sufficient lower bound turns negative: this is a limitation of the bound, NOT a DPP counterexample. For any fixed rho in (0,1), G_epsilon(rho)->-b(rho). This sandwich alone cannot settle the high-contrast bulk-density problem.

## The acceleration obligation is not removed

Define

    A_n(a)=-sum_x p''_x(a)log p_x(a),
    H_n''(a)=A_n(a)-F_n(a).

F2-F3 settle the centered complete-law Fisher density, not A_n/n. A finite-word log likelihood is itself concave along the scalar shift, because d_a^2 log p_x=-Tr(K_n-I_Zx)^-2<0, but that is not the entropy curvature: A_n remains in the latter.

A valid sufficient continuation is the following. On a fixed strict a interval, if one proves for all n>=n0 and all a in that interval

    A_n(a)/n <= 1/[v(a,c,rho)+c^2 V_n/n]-gamma

with one gamma>0, then F1 implies (H_n/n)''<=-gamma. Pointwise passage of the resulting Jensen inequalities proves that h_E(a,c)+gamma a^2/2 is concave. This does not require differentiating the entropy-rate limit. The required all-n acceleration estimate has NOT been proved or numerically certified here.
