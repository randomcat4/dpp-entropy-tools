# Assumptions and boundaries

- Strict finite real symmetric contractions give positive complete-event
  probabilities and smooth entropy in the interior.
- The original observed coordinates are fixed. Only explicitly justified
  diagonal sign conjugation or labelled coordinate permutation is used.
- Rank, inertia, support and reducing-sector hypotheses are distinct.
- PR43's imported diagonal-anchor theorem is bound to PR29's exact source
  commit; its hypotheses must be matched, not inferred from a prior verdict.
- Boundary entropy values extend by continuity; interior second-derivative
  formulas do not automatically extend to events of zero probability.
- A finite exact example certifies that example, not a quantified theorem.
- Mathematical correctness, formalization and novelty have separate statuses.
- Author files are immutable to this review. Reviewers are non-authors;
  independent second reviewers do not receive the initial verdict as proof.
