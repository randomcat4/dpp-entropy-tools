# Verification question

Do the new PR41 and PR43 claims follow, with their stated quantifiers, from
their complete-configuration DPP probabilities and actual kernel-affine paths?

The object is Shannon entropy of all labelled occupied subsets. For a strict
real symmetric contraction K, the event law is recovered from every principal
minor by inclusion–exclusion. Entropy derivatives use every event's Fisher
term and second derivative. Spectral entropy, particle-count entropy,
unjustified observed-coordinate rotations and a projected Fisher norm cannot
replace this object.

This bounded task audits existing proofs and fixed certificates. It does not
seek a new general theorem or run a new open-ended search.
