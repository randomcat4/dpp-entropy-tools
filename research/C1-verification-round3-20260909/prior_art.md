# External dependencies; novelty not reviewed

The [DPP monograph](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.6083) is background for the
labelled determinantal law, not a citation proving the new entropy claims.

Dierckx, Fannes and Pogorzelska,
[Fermionic Quasi-free States and Maps in Information Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.1061),
section 5.1 and Proposition 8, gives the completely positive, trace-preserving
quasi-free map with symbol Q ↦ A*QA+B under 0 ≤ B ≤ I−A*A. Choosing
A=√θ I and B=(1−θ)A0 verifies existence of the contraction used in PR43 N
for 0≤θ≤1 and 0≤A0≤I. This external check concerns the quantum map only;
it does not imply a classical occupation channel or a Shannon-entropy theorem.
The actual paper title is recorded here for precise attribution.

No novelty or priority search was performed. Correctness acceptance must not
be reworded as a claim of firstness or unrestricted new theory.
