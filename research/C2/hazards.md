# Domain risks and checks

- Complete configuration entropy is used, not cardinality or quantum entropy. All 26 positive events appear; six larger events are identically zero.
- Every derivative uses A+tV and therefore K+tUVU^T. Eigenbases are used to compute differentials at a point, not to replace the path by a spectral interpolation.
- Repeated eigenvalues are allowed; differential identities are polynomial and extend without divided-eigenvalue singularities.
- Noncommuting directions are retained. Commutation in the pair-null theorem is a derived conclusion, not an imposed assumption.
- Rare-event logarithms are retained and canceled only through the exact weighted second-derivative identity. The scaled probabilities have explicit positive lower bounds in each claimed range.
- Uniform estimates apply to all V, including V depending on the approach parameter. Fixed-B expansions do not silently claim uniformity at singular B.
- Positive top geometry is not a counterexample. All Fisher and log terms are reported by layer and summed.
- No positive chord was found; no common interior lift is claimed. Negative finite certificates remain negative examples only.
- Numeric Hessian signs at finitely many centers are exploratory. Exact rational frame constants and interval finite-chord signs have their own validation scope.
- Reviewers did not author the proof they audit. Source formula calibration and proof review are distinct objects.
- No proof-assistant certification of the entropy inequalities is claimed; standard-library Lean artifacts inherited elsewhere concern different statements.
