# Addendum — two-margin joint centering, low-rank normal equations, and a strict insufficiency witness

Status: **PROVED / DISPROVED only at the scoped statements below (author proof), not independently reviewed**.

This continues PR #58 from current main. It uses the accepted rank-two full-law form

\[
q_s(S,T)=1-s a(S,T)+s^2 b(S,T),\qquad P_s=\mu q_s,\qquad \mu=p_A\otimes p_C,
\]

with `s=t^2`, `u=q_s-1`, `y=s^2b`, and the accepted outer-wedge identity

\[
t^2 I''(t)=E_\mu\left[\Phi(u)+\frac{4y^2}{q_s}+y\psi(u)\right],
\]

where `Phi(u)=4u^2/(1+u)+2u log(1+u)` and `psi(u)=8u/(1+u)+10log(1+u)`. Every complete event and the full Fisher term are retained.

## 1. Exact two-margin Hilbert projection

Put

\[
h(S,T)=\frac{y(S,T)}{q_s(S,T)}.
\]

Because both block marginals of `P_s` are fixed and the exterior coefficient has zero conditional mean on either block,

\[
E_{P_s}[h\mid S]=\sum_T p_C(T)y(S,T)=0,
\qquad
E_{P_s}[h\mid T]=\sum_S p_A(S)y(S,T)=0. \tag{1.1}
\]

Let `L^2(P_s)` carry its usual inner product and let

\[
\mathcal A=\{f(S)+g(T)\}
\]

be the additive subspace. Then (1.1) says `h` is orthogonal to `mathcal A`. Therefore for every `f,g`,

\[
E_\mu[y\psi(u)]
=E_{P_s}[h\psi(u)]
=E_{P_s}[h(\psi(u)-f-g)]. \tag{1.2}
\]

Define the **joint additive residual**

\[
R_{\rm add}(s)=\inf_{f,g}E_{P_s}[(\psi(u_s)-f(S)-g(T))^2]. \tag{1.3}
\]

Weighted Cauchy–Schwarz and `A_2=4E_mu[y^2/q_s]=4E_{P_s}h^2` give

\[
\boxed{
 t^2I''(t)\ge P(s)+A_2(s)-\frac12\sqrt{A_2(s)R_{\rm add}(s)},
}\tag{1.4}
\]

where `P(s)=E_mu Phi(u_s)`. This is no weaker than either one-sided conditional-centering bound from the previous PR58 commit, since the additive subspace contains the left-only and right-only subspaces.

### Normal equations and gauge freedom

Let `c=E_{P_s} psi`, `psi_0=psi-c`, and use zero-mean spaces

\[
U=L^2_0(p_A),\qquad V=L^2_0(p_C).
\]

Define the conditional expectation operator

\[
(K_sg)(S)=E_{P_s}[g(T)\mid S],\qquad K_s^*:U\to V.
\]

Write

\[
m(S)=E_{P_s}[\psi_0\mid S],\qquad n(T)=E_{P_s}[\psi_0\mid T].
\]

Choosing the gauge `E f=E g=0` (the original representation has the freedom `f->f+c0`, `g->g-c0`), the unique minimizing pair satisfies

\[
\boxed{f+K_sg=m,\qquad g+K_s^*f=n.}\tag{1.5}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
(I-K_sK_s^*)f=m-K_sn,
\qquad
(I-K_s^*K_s)g=n-K_s^*m. \tag{1.6}
\]

For a strict finite DPP all entries of `P_s` are positive. Hence `||K_s||_{U\to U}<1`: equality in the conditional Jensen contraction would force a nonconstant function of `S` to equal a nonconstant function of `T` on the full Cartesian support, which is impossible. Thus (1.6) is invertible. The residual itself is unique even before fixing the gauge.

A convenient exact value formula is

\[
R_{\rm add}=E_{P_s}[\psi_0^2]-\langle m,f\rangle_{p_A}-\langle n,g\rangle_{p_C}. \tag{1.7}
\]

## 2. DPP-specific rank-four reduction of the projection system

This projection is not a generic 15-dimensional black box. For rank two write

\[
a(S,T)=\operatorname{tr}(G_A(S)G_C(T)),\qquad
b(S,T)=\alpha(S)\beta(T),
\]

with `alpha=det G_A`, `beta=det G_C`. For a centered right function `g`, fixed marginal cancellation gives

\[
(K_sg)(S)
=-s\,\operatorname{tr}\left(G_A(S)\,E_{p_C}[G_Cg]\right)
+s^2\alpha(S)E_{p_C}[\beta g]. \tag{2.1}
\]

Thus

\[
\operatorname{rank}K_s\le4, \tag{2.2}
\]

and its range is contained in the four-dimensional feature space generated by the three entries of `G_A` and `det G_A`; symmetrically for `K_s^*`. Consequently the nontrivial part of (1.6) reduces to at most four feature coordinates per side. Components of `m` orthogonal to `Ran K_s` pass through unchanged. This is the precise DPP structural gain from the two-margin projection.

The reduction also shows why a universal visible Markov generator is not needed: the projection uses only the exact low-rank likelihood and fixed marginals. Conversely, it does not turn (1.4) into a necessary condition.

## 4. Comparison with the nonreversible / hidden-state route

Erbar–Maas, *Ricci curvature of finite Markov chains via convexity of the entropy*, arXiv:1111.2687, defines its entropy-geodesic curvature for an **irreducible reversible** finite Markov kernel and for geodesics of the paper's nonlocal transport metric. The associated heat semigroup is a gradient flow in that metric. Those hypotheses do not identify our physical radial parameter `t` (or `tau=-log s`) with an Erbar–Maas transport geodesic, and they do not cover the generally nonreversible directed generator sought here.

Therefore even the already accepted existence of one fixed correlated three-point nonreversible exterior-scaling generator does not imply

\[
2\mathcal I''+\mathcal I'\ge0
\]

along the DPP radial density trajectory. A hidden-state dilation likewise needs an exact visible intertwining plus a proof that the required second-order entropy-production inequality descends after projection. The joint-projection route is structurally different: it uses only fixed marginals and the rank-four DPP likelihood operator, but Section 3 proves it too is only sufficient.

Primary source: M. Erbar and J. Maas, arXiv:1111.2687 (2012 revision), especially the reversible-kernel setup and geodesic entropy-convexity definition.
