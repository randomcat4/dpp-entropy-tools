# Frozen N4 target v1

Status: frozen by route owner. Proof and review agents may not change premises.

## Objects and intent
For every integer n >= 4, K and D are real symmetric n by n matrices.
K is a strict positive contraction, and t>0 must satisfy 0<K-tD,K+tD<I.
The full event law is
`p_K(S)=(-1)^(n-|S|) det(K-diag(1_{i notin S}))`.
Equivalently, inclusion principal minors are converted by Mobius inversion.
`H(K)=-sum_S p_K(S) log(p_K(S))`, with natural logarithms.
The source task is finite real full-configuration entropy concavity; neither
cardinality entropy nor stationary entropy rate is a replacement target.

## Claim under investigation
For all such objects, `Delta=(H(K-tD)+H(K+tD))/2-H(K)<=0`.
Equivalently on the open domain, `H''(K)[D,D]<=0` for every D.
There is no commuting, definiteness, equal-diagonal or fixed-frame assumption.
The first finite search scope is n=4, then evidence-driven n=5 through 8.

## Success gate
A disproof requires a frozen real rational K,D,t, exact strict endpoint
feasibility, and rigorous positive entropy-gap bounds. A floating Hessian
eigenvalue alone is only a candidate. A structural theorem must state its
scope, prove all quantifiers and receive independent non-author review.
The unrestricted open conclusion requires two fresh non-author reviewers.

## Boundaries and interfaces
All final chords are affine in K; nonlinear spectral or L parameter paths
only generate midpoint candidates. Gauge transformations by diagonal signs
are identified; unrelated cycle-sign classes are retained. No random coupling
or independence assumption is available beyond the exact DPP law.
Numerical zeros near products and cancellation at vanishing event masses
must pass an independent arithmetic path. Finite coverage proves no global
claim. Novelty remains unconfirmed pending source audit.
