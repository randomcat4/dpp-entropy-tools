# Failure and limitation ledger for the strong-middle continuation

No item below is an entropy counterexample unless explicitly accompanied by a legal exact three-kernel Jensen certificate with positive counterexample-oriented gap. None is.

## 1. Local matched-Schur result was not new after current review comparison

The previously local common-coordinate matched-Schur axis proof is mathematically valid, but the latest PR88 bounded review already accepts a more general theorem: an indefinite rank-two direction supported on three actual observation coordinates is concave on its full legal chord. The local axis was therefore reclassified as a specialization, retaining only its new quantitative Fisher bound. It is not presented as an independent main theorem.

## 2. PR86 publication defect, not a failed theorem

Frozen PR86 promised `full_square_certificate.json` but omitted that file, and its README did not map four later theorem files. ## 3. Structured strong-endpoint diagnostics did not yield positive full curvature

A bounded, deterministic mechanism probe varied strong logical spectra, rational principal angles and rational top/bottom rotations for disjoint rank-two support planes. It evaluated all sixteen complete atoms and the full midpoint expression

\[
H''=-\sum_S(p'_S)^2/p_S-\sum_Sp''_S\log p_S.
\]

Among 8904 structured points, 1352 had positive total acceleration `-sum p'' log p`; none had positive total `H''`. These counts were used only to select an exact rational candidate with a visibly positive acceleration term. They are not a theorem, a random-exclusion claim, or a substitute for the later interval proof.

## 4. Gauge-flat sign-conjugate endpoints

For a diagonal sign matrix `J`, endpoint pairs `X` and `JXJ` have equal endpoint laws, while their midpoint deletes selected cross blocks. At the exact sign-conjugate midpoint the complete score vanishes and the radial cross-coupling entropy curvature is second-order flat; the first known strict loss is quartic. This initially appeared dangerous because the Fisher term vanishes at the center.

Small rank-two-manifold tangent probes around several strong examples produced a negative-semidefinite numerical quadratic form, apart from the expected gauge redundancy. This diagnostic was not upgraded to a local theorem. The exact sign-conjugate line is already a fixed-block cross-coupling structure, and broad perturbation scanning was stopped rather than enlarged after no positive quadratic direction appeared.

## 5. Walsh/Hadamard orthogonal-support family

A second structured route used

U=2^{-1/2}\binom{I_2}{I_2},\qquad
V=2^{-1/2}\binom{I_2}{-I_2}

with strongly correlated logical rank-two kernels. A deterministic 2916-point diagnostic over spectra and internal angles found no positive complete curvature. Equal logical kernels reduce to the gauge-flat structure. This is not a family theorem and no finite no-hit statement is used downstream.

## 6. Two certificate implementation failures were kept separate from mathematics

The first two-parameter strong-family implementation repeatedly formed large exact rational logarithm denominators and exceeded short execution caps. It produced no retained sign evidence.

A faster implementation initially used natural monomial intervals on a coarse right-end rectangle. One regular factor received a negative lower enclosure even though its exact corner values were positive. This was an interval dependency failure, not a zero event or a mathematical counterexample. The final checker keeps the same family and replaces that step by exact tensor-product Bernstein coefficients plus 192-bit directed fixed-point logarithms. It then proves all regular factors positive.

## 7. What the completed strong-family theorem does and does not say

The exact one-chord proof and its endpoint-strength extension establish a genuine harmful acceleration mechanism:

- at the full-rank midpoint the acceleration is strictly positive;
- it remains positive for every endpoint high eigenvalue in `[4/5,9/10]`;
- nevertheless a physical one-coordinate statistic supplies a uniform complete-Fisher lower bound, proving `H''<-1/2` on every entire chord in the family.

The proof therefore excludes this natural strong, substantially Schur-mismatched family rather than merely reporting finite negative samples.

Its main limitation is equally explicit: the direction has a large diagonal entry, approximately `-0.85`, so one visible occupancy bit already forces large Fisher information. The remaining serious class is a strong disjoint-support rank-four direction whose diagonal entries—and preferably the midpoint derivatives of all pair and triple inclusion indicators—are simultaneously small. Positive acceleration in such a low-visible-Fisher family is not controlled by the present theorem.
