# Failure and scope ledger

## Routes attempted

1. **Fixed five-point dangerous seed, enlarged by frame tangents.**  The saved
   N4/F2 seed with positive top-layer curvature was used as the conceptual
   starting point.  Local tangent probes that also move the physical frame did
   not produce positive complete curvature.  This was stopped rather than
   expanded into another random scan; the result is not a theorem.

2. **Fast moving rank-one endpoints.**  Instead of treating a nonlinear
   `U(t)A(t)U(t)^T` curve as affine, two explicit endpoints were formed first
   and their true matrix midpoint was analyzed.  This route closed in the
   opposite direction: Theorem 1 proves strict midpoint concavity for the
   entire family, and Theorem 2 gives explicit strict interior lifts.

3. **Strongly correlated signed 3+3 triangles.**  A dense rank-two coupling
   outside the PR43 special null-eigenvector family was frozen.  Exact legality
   and all 64 atom polynomials were obtained.  Twelve motivated points from
   `t=1/10` through `21/10` had negative 110-digit curvature and negative true
   local Jensen gaps, despite a positive cardinality-three layer.  This is an
   informative compensation failure, not a family exclusion.

4. **Direct rational log-interval evaluation for the whole compact middle.**
   A naive exact-fraction atanh implementation suffered severe denominator
   growth before completing the requested interval task.  No output from that
   aborted implementation is retained as evidence.  The compact interval
   certificate has instead been specified for C2/local-agent execution with
   exact inputs, adaptive subdivision, and explicit stop conditions.

## Hazards explicitly avoided

- No principal inclusion minor is called an exact event probability.
- No nonlinear moving-frame curve is called a `K`-affine chord.
- No step is taken outward from a PSD rank-deficient face; strict kernels are
  obtained by the explicit affine bit-flip lift.
- No floating-point negative sample is promoted to a universal theorem.
- No positive cardinality layer is promoted to positive full entropy
  curvature.
- No fixed-five frame result is treated as invariant under spectral rotation.
- No same-session numerical self-check is called independent review.

## Remaining mathematical gaps

The unrestricted real-kernel problem remains open in this work unit.  The new
proved exclusion covers moving rank-one endpoint chords and sufficiently small
common bit-flip lifts, but not rank-two or rank-three endpoints.  The frozen
3+3 line still needs a continuous interval certificate; even a successful
certificate for that line would not settle arbitrary correlated blocks.
