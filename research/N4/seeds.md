# Distinct seeds and decisions

1. All labeled gauge-inequivalent K4/diamond signs with unequal coordinates:
   36-kernel pilot, no positive signal; weak curvature was a missing-edge mode.
2. Moving eigenspaces and unequal spectral slack scales: 160-kernel full-Hessian
   study with adaptive ratio refinement, no positive signal; near-product
   controls were rejected as a mechanism.
3. Diamond two-column coupling: exact mixed conditional-Hessian identity,
   followed by a 24-kernel rare-scale/soft-column diagnostic.
4. Strong-coupling conditional gain: bounded targeted refinement to distinguish
   shared boundary Fisher modes from an actual positive-curvature direction.
5. Analytic exclusions: separated-scale cross-term suppression under explicit
   single-column nondegeneracy; joint central-block and radial-column concavity.

The last two analytic objects have different premises and must not be merged
into an unrestricted theorem. General rotations, changing leaf marginals and
the missing leaf edge remain outside the radial theorem.
