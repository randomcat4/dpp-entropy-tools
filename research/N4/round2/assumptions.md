# Explicit premises and scope

- Observed coordinates are fixed. U is a fixed real isometry for each face;
  an orthogonal rotation of observed coordinates is not an invariance of H.
- A and V are real symmetric 3 by 3 matrices. Centers and endpoints satisfy
  0<A,A-tV,A+tV<I_3. General noncommuting V is permitted.
- Four-point faces have every null-vector coordinate nonzero. Five-point
  frames have every three-row minor nonzero. These are support assumptions,
  not universal assumptions on the original strict-kernel target.
- H uses exact configuration probabilities. Structural zeros and all their
  face derivatives are deleted before logarithms and divisions.
- A rational counterexample certificate would require exact feasibility,
  exact event probabilities and rigorous logarithm enclosures. High-precision
  floating-point scouts do not meet this standard.
- The BSC transfer premise is an already certified positive boundary gap g.
  The conditional lift lemma does not supply such a g.
- Fixed scalar A controls are retained and labelled. Finite probes and
  noncommuting fixtures do not quantify over all U,A,V.
- The proof authors must not change the frozen premises. Exploratory n=5
  scope was separately frozen as v2; v1 remains unaltered.
