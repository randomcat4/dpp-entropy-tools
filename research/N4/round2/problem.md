# Fixed noncoordinate boundary faces

The parent problem is concavity of the full configuration Shannon entropy of
finite DPPs with real symmetric strict marginal kernel 0<K<I. A counterexample
has Delta=(H(K-tD)+H(K+tD))/2-H(K)>0 on a strict feasible affine chord.
All logarithms are natural. Cardinality entropy and spectral Bernoulli entropy
are different objects and cannot replace the full entropy.

Round two first fixes a real 4 by 3 isometry U with a null vector whose four
coordinates are nonzero, sets K=UAU^T, and varies all six symmetric coordinates
of 0<A<I_3. The full event and its derivatives are identically zero.
A strict positive face gap would transfer by the common BSC shrink
K_j(e)=eI+(1-2e)K_j, using one rational e for the entire chord.

The bounded second unit fixes a full-spark real 5 by 3 U. It was authorized
only after an explicit maximum-cardinality projection entropy gate was
independently certified. Its support consists of the 26 subsets of size at
most three; all six larger subsets are structural zeros.
