# Sources and boundaries

Coordinator's source-by-source reassessment is recorded in public PR #25.
The parent re-opened HKPV, [Determinantal Processes and Independence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0503110),
Theorem 7: a finite DPP is a Bernoulli mixture of projection DPPs in its actual
eigenfunctions. This supports support reasoning but neither identifies full
configuration entropy with cardinality entropy nor permits a free rotation of
observation coordinates. We prove the finite support fact directly in this round.

Real principal-minor constraints and information-geometric parameterizations
in the reassessment do not assert concavity along K-affine chords. No new
literature completeness or novelty claim is made. The single coordinator
preflight is a negative execution check only, not independently reviewed proof.
