# Hazards
- Inclusion minors are not exact-event probabilities.
- A straight line in L or rotation parameters is generally curved in K.
- Cycle signs must be quotiented by vertex sign gauge.
- Vanishing masses and inverse formulas can produce cancellation artifacts.
- A Fisher numerical nullspace cutoff can discard relevant directions.
- A normalized direction needs its own strict feasible radius.
- Fixed-data boundary theorems cannot be substituted into moving data without uniform control.
- Neither a finite non-hit nor a stable decimal value is a universal or interval proof.
