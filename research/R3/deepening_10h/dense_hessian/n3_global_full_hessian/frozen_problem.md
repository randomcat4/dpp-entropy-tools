# U8/M10 frozen global n=3 questions

Author research, not self-certified.

Let K range over all real symmetric 3x3 matrices with 0<K<I. Let H(K) be the Shannon entropy, using natural logarithms, of its full eight-event DPP law. Exact events are computed by Mobius inversion of inclusion determinants. Let B(K)=-Hess H(K), in the six observation coordinates (11,22,33,12,13,23), where each off-diagonal coordinate means E_ij+E_ji.

Question G1: is B(K) positive semidefinite for EVERY such K?

Question G2: is B(K) positive definite whenever the observation support graph (edge ij iff K_ij!=0) is connected?

Positive definiteness without a connectedness condition is already false: at a strict diagonal kernel, all three off-diagonal Hessian directions are exactly flat. More generally a block-diagonal kernel has an entire cross-block Hessian-flat subspace. These are degeneracies, not positive-curvature counterexamples to G1. In dimension three, a connected support is either a two-edge path or a triangle; zero individual entries do not automatically imply a flat direction.

The study must distinguish global assertions, proved conditional subclasses or structural identities, and finite scouts. A positive full curvature witness requires strict feasibility, exact-event jets, a rational or high-precision sign gate, and frozen K,D. Finite failure to find one leaves G1/G2 INCOMPLETE.
