# U10a result and blocker

GLOBAL rho(K)<=1: INCOMPLETE. No strict real-DPP positive-curvature candidate
was produced. The author does not self-certify these new lemmas.

New candidate results are:

1. Connected n=3 implies positive-definite event Fisher on all Sym(3), including
   path kernels. The proof uses the exact inclusion Jacobian, not a numerical
   rank or nonzero eigenvalue test.
2. An exact eight-event regularized projection identity for rho. Its
   unregularized trace capacity has a one-dimensional inclusion-statistic
   minimization formula and the explicit sufficient test (2det(N)/3)V<=1.
3. A four by four sufficient certificate obtained by grouping eight events
   into five categories, plus its complement-adaptive version. All formulas
   are explicit in atom values/gradients and conditional odds logs.
4. An analytic obstruction: the trace-capacity test grows like
   (2theta/3)log(1/e) at a reviewed boundary where the true rho approaches one
   from below. The fixed-orientation five-category certificate similarly
   tends to three at the complementary boundary. These are counterexamples
   to proof shortcuts, not counterexamples to DPP concavity.
5. The adaptive five-category certificate covers sufficiently small portions
   of each reviewed fixed dense rank-one boundary family and its complement;
   the correct orientation has the same first rho deficit 1/(theta log(1/e)).
6. Universal applicability of even the adaptive five-category certificate
   is disproved at the rational path with diagonal 1/2 and path edges 3/10.
   Exact rational log intervals give surrogate B_T(D)<-1.048 but actual
   B(D)>28.680 in the explicit positive-definite rational direction.
   Complement/sign-conjugation gives the second orientation's failure.
7. The omitted conditional Fisher information is restored exactly as three
   positive rank-one orthogonal score contrasts. Their Sherman–Morrison
   scalar corrections reduce the remaining global question to the explicit
   inequality c1+c2+c3>=R_T-1 whenever R_T>1.

The adaptive four by four inequality is now known to be too strong; it
cannot close the global proof. The exact projection bound, equivalently the
three-contrast correction inequality, remains the minimal unresolved step.
Retaining the full-versus-singletons contrast gives a six-category sufficient
condition, and rescues the explicit path obstruction, but no global
guarantee is inferred from its 29 finite sanity passes.

The full-domain quantifiers are retained in the identities and sufficient
conditions. The boundary subclass fixes theta in (0,1) and a unit vector u
with all coordinates nonzero; thresholds are allowed to depend on both.
There is no uniform gap, no fixed-eigenvector restriction on Hessian
directions, and no assertion covering a simultaneous degeneration of those
fixed parameters.
