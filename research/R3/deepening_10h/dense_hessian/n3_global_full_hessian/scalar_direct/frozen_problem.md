# D10-U10a frozen problem

For every real symmetric strict 3 by 3 kernel K with connected nonzero
off-diagonal support, retain the reviewed U8 definitions of the eight exact
atoms, event Fisher form F, positive matrix N, delta=det N, and
rho=delta eta^T(F+delta G_N)^(-1)eta. The objective is rho<=1 on this entire
domain; strict rho<1 would prove full-Hessian strict negativity.

No restrictions on spectrum, signs, diagonal entries, or direction commutation
are introduced. Disconnected points are excluded only from inverse-N formulas.
The reviewed rank-one boundary family has rho tending to one from below, so
no positive uniform gap may be assumed.

This unit studies event-score projections, exact Fisher estimator capacity,
and five-category coarse graining. A computable sufficient subclass is not a
solution of the global problem. All calculations are bounded sanity checks,
not a search denominator proving a theorem. Author candidates remain pending
independent review. Only this scalar_direct directory is writable.
