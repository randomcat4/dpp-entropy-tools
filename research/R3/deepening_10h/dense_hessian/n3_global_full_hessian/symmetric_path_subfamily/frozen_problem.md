# D10-U10d frozen problem

For K(x,a)=[[x,a,0],[a,x,a],[0,a,x]], determine whether the full negative
entropy Hessian B=-Hess H is positive definite on Sym(3) whenever

0<x<1, 0<sqrt(2)|a|<min(x,1-x).

H is the Shannon entropy of all eight exact events obtained by Möbius
inversion of inclusion minors. Directions range over all six real-symmetric
coordinates, not merely the two parameters x,a, and need not commute with K.

Diagonal sign conjugation permits a>0; complementation together with sign
conjugation permits x<=1/2 for analysis. Any result confined to x=1/2 must
be distinguished from the original full two-parameter assertion. Boundary
a=0 is disconnected and can have flat Hessian directions; spectral boundary
points are excluded. The author does not self-certify correctness.
