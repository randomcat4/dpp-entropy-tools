# D10-U10i: the exponential full-atom boundary limit

AUTHOR RESEARCH; new claims are proof candidates pending independent review.

Consider K=[[x,a,0],[a,x,a],[0,a,x]],
a=x sqrt((1-s)/2), s=exp(-beta/x), beta>0, x decreases to zero.
Here beta is an exponential rate, NOT an eigenvalue coordinate from the
exchangeable-triangle units. For 0<x<1/2 and 0<s<1 the kernel is strict.

The target scalar sigma is exactly the reviewed weighted-trace Schur
complement, with transverse vector e0=(0,1,0,0) in even coordinates
(d,e,h,k). The normalization must not be changed: rescaling e0 would
rescale the limiting scalar.

Goals: derive phi(beta)=lim sigma(x,exp(-beta/x)) analytically; settle its
sign for every fixed beta>0; establish an honest uniform remainder scope.
No finite profile can prove either the limit or the sign.

Inputs: [path reduction](../derivation.md) and
[previous stable formula](../boundary_full_atom/analysis.md).
No claim about the entire two-parameter path domain or about arbitrary
beta=beta(x) tending to zero/infinity is implicit in the fixed-rate result.
