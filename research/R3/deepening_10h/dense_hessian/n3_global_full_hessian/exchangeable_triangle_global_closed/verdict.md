# D10-U10k verdict

`CORRECT` for the full strict connected exchangeable-triangle family.

For

\[
K=xI+a(J-I),\qquad
\alpha=x+2a,\quad\beta=x-a,
\]

if \(0<\alpha,\beta<1\) and \(\alpha\ne\beta\), then the Shannon entropy
Hessian is strictly negative definite on all of `Sym(3)`.

The previously open invariant determinant has the form

\[
\Delta_T=
\frac{\sum_{k=0}^4c_k(t)q^k}
{qt(1+q)^2(t+2)(2t+1)},
\]

where \(q>0\), \(t>0\), \(t\ne1\), and every coefficient \(c_k(t)\) is
strictly positive.  This closes the separated compact middle and all four
simultaneous corner regimes left open by U10h.

On the disconnected diagonal \(\alpha=\beta=x\), the invariant block has
the exact null direction \((2,-1)\).  Along the corresponding fixed-\(x\)
puncture the quadratic term vanishes, but the quartic derivative is

\[
\partial_a^4H(x,0)=-36/[x^2(1-x)^2]<0.
\]

Therefore the near-zero numerical values around this locus come from a
genuine positive-dimensional second-order ridge with strict quartic descent,
not an isolated sign-changing point.

This theorem does not prove the general connected \(n=3\) scalar gate
\(\rho<1\), the general symmetric-path gate, the entire connected
\(\Lambda=0\) submanifold, or the all-dimensional conjecture.
