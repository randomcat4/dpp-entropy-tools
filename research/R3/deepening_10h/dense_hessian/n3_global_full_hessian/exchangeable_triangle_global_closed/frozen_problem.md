# D10-U10k: global exchangeable-triangle determinant gate

Let

\[
K(x,a)=xI+a(J-I),\qquad
\alpha=x+2a,\quad \beta=x-a.
\]

Strict feasibility is exactly

\[
0<\alpha<1,\qquad 0<\beta<1.
\]

The support is connected exactly when \(a\ne0\), equivalently
\(\alpha\ne\beta\).  The reviewed U10f reduction proves that the
four-dimensional standard representation block of
\(B=-\operatorname{Hess}H\) is strictly positive on this connected domain.
The remaining invariant block is a two-by-two matrix \(C(\alpha,\beta)\),
and the exact unresolved gate inherited from U10h was

\[
\Delta_T(\alpha,\beta):=\det C(\alpha,\beta)>0.
\]

The frozen task is to prove this inequality on the entire strict square
off the diagonal, without a finite grid or a compact-boundary reduction.
The diagonal must be treated separately: it is a genuine disconnected
second-order degeneracy and is not part of the strict-positivity claim.
