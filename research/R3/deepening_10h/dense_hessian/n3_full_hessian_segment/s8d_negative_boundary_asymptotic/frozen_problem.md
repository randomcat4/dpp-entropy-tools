Author research; no self-certification. Frozen target: prove or refute full Sym(3) entropy Hessian negative definiteness on the continuous interval -1<t<=-29/100. This is not merely curvature in the direction of the line.

K(t)=(1/5+t/5)U+(1/2+t/3)V+(4/5+2t/3)W.

K=(s/5)U+(1/6+s/3)V+(2/15+2s/3)W.

(13/18,289/3780,79/945,1/135,361/3780,1/135,1/135,0).

p_{123}(s)=s/225+(7/225)s^2+(2/45)s^3.

The endpoint s=0 is excluded, because an atom vanishes and the ambient full entropy Hessian is singular there. No uniform spectral margin or full open neighborhood through this endpoint is claimed.
