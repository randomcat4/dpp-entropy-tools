# D10-U verdict

Author status: `PROVED`; fresh verification is still required before `CORRECT`.

The zero Hessian observed at (K=I/2) in off-diagonal directions is completely
classified.  It is a genuine flat point at second order, but not a flat
neighborhood: every fixed nonzero zero-diagonal ray loses entropy at fourth
order with explicit coefficient

\[
-8\sum_{i<j}D_{ij}^4.
\]

The radial second derivative is consequently strictly negative at all
sufficiently close nonzero points on the ray.  Near-zero float Hessian values
generated by approaching this center should therefore be treated as a known
quartic-flat negative mechanism, not promoted to positive candidates without
a separately resolved positive leading coefficient.

This route does not prove concavity on a full neighborhood in all directions
and does not settle the R3 conjecture.
