# Hazards

- Inclusion minors are not complete event probabilities.
- Counterexample sign is endpoints minus midpoint, strictly positive.
- All endpoints and centers must satisfy strict real spectral feasibility.
- Triangle sign product is gauge invariant; arbitrary orthogonal changes are
  not entropy symmetries.
- A symmetric center does not restrict the direction to its symmetry class.
- Nonaffine conditioning contributes a second-derivative acceleration term.
- Strict internal positivity is required for logarithmic derivatives.
- Zero second derivative need not mean a flat finite chord.
- Small floating eigenvalues and finite searches are not mathematical signs.
- A reduction to an equivalent inequality is a blocker, not a solved lemma.
- Changed assumptions require a separate frozen version.
- Verification must bind exact Git objects, not moving worktree content.
