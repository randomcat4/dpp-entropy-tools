# Assumptions

- K is real symmetric, dimension exactly 3, with 0<K<I.
- Connectedness means the graph of nonzero off-diagonal entries is connected.
- A triangle has all three off-diagonal entries nonzero; signed diagonal
  conjugation and permutations preserve entropy but not arbitrary rotations.
- Chords K0 +/- tV have real symmetric V, t>0, and strictly feasible endpoints.
- Every exact event is reconstructed by inclusion-exclusion. Natural logs.
- Strict interior implies positive exact event probabilities.
- Conditional two-point kernels vary non-affinely; their acceleration term
  cannot be discarded when applying a chain rule.
- No stationarity, entropy rate, high-dimensional grouped-kernel route, or
  fixed-projection boundary limit is part of this work unit.
