# What the new objects cover

| Object | Centers | Directions / chords | What remains outside |
|---|---|---|---|
| Radial slice | Any strict K=[[A,sv],[sv^T,c]] with fixed c,v | All feasible changes of A and s; four parameters; strict finite midpoint inequality | Variation of c or rotation of the two coupling entries relative to v |
| Standard modes | K_r=(1/2)I+r(J-I), abs(r)<1/4 | All four dimensions tr(V)=0, V12+V13+V23=0; local Hessian | Remaining two symmetric modes, nonsymmetric centers, finite chords away from these centers |
| Acceleration obstruction | One explicit rational connected triangle | Positive conditional acceleration in a specified transverse coupling direction | Does not give a positive total Hessian or chord gap |

At a connected center one may select a site with nonzero coupling vector and
take that vector as v and s=1. The radial theorem therefore gives a
four-dimensional affine slice through that center, without requiring the
midpoint to split into blocks. It does not prove the full six-dimensional
Hessian is negative semidefinite.

For half-filled symmetric centers, the analytic decomposition plus the
standard-mode result leaves exactly the displayed 2 by 2 trivial block T.
Its two diagonal signs and determinant sign are an equivalent local blocker
only within that one-parameter center family. They are not an equivalent
reduction of the entire general three-point problem.
