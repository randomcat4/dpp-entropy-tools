# Problem

Study finite DPP full-event Shannon entropy for connected strict real
symmetric 3 by 3 kernels, emphasizing triangle cycles and all six real
symmetric tangent coordinates. The intended milestone is a rigorously
certified real counterexample, a precisely quantified three-dimensional
subclass theorem, or a checkable minimal core gap. Random finite non-hits
are not a theorem. R1's two-dimensional and block exclusions are background,
not new deliverables.
