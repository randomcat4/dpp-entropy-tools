# Audit hazards

- A saved-output audit is not a fresh production reproduction.
- `leaves=4^9` counts future words; four current events are evaluated inside
  every leaf, giving `4^10` current/future atoms.
- Taylor-jet `h` stores the coefficient of `dt^2`, not the second derivative.
- The per-original-coordinate factor `1/2` must cancel the factor two that
  converts the Taylor coefficient to a second derivative.
- Node checks alone do not cover a closed cell without the DCT and ellipse
  error terms.
- Publication repairs and the pre-arithmetic SyntaxError cannot be relabeled
  as successful historical runs.
- The unavailable compiler blocks the mandatory fresh production gate.
