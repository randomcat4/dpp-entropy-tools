# Source and scope audit

Checked 2026-09-09, limited to the supplied route and its direct sources.

- [Lyons–Steif original arXiv text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204324), Conjecture 9.2: fixed measurable scalar symbols and dynamical entropy. Section 6, especially its conditional-entropy argument and Theorem 6.12, supplies a possible rate-enclosure mechanism. The theorem is not itself an explicit floating-error certificate or a derivative-limit interchange.
- [Author-maintained publication page](https://rdlyons.pages.iu.edu/), corresponding entry: links a 1 June 2025 version. Direct PDF retrieval timed out twice. The original arXiv copy was read successfully.
- [Author's errata dated 29 November 2025](https://rdlyons.pages.iu.edu/errata/dyn.pdf): corrections concern Remarks 5.12 and 7.16, not the entropy theorem used here.
- [Mészáros, Limiting entropy of determinantal processes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11459): supplied alternative representation; no theorem from it has been applied in the baseline.
- Public T1 bridge-direction theorem is restricted to existing graph bridges; multiharmonic Toeplitz cycle edges need separate analysis. Public T2 block loss inequality supplies the coarse bound in main/coarse_rate_bounds.md.

Queries for the exact conjecture name and stationary entropy concavity returned the original work and author-hosted expositions, without a directly applicable solution. This is a limited status check, not a proof of continued openness or a novelty certification. None of the finite tests establishes a result for the full symbol class.
