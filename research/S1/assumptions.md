# Assumptions

- Original target: fixed measurable f,g:T->[0,1]; scalar, real-valued but not necessarily even; normalized circle measure; natural-log exact configuration entropy.
- First diagnostic subfamily: fixed rational finite trigonometric coefficients, affine sine perturbations, uniform positive margin. This restriction is explicit and not imposed on the original conjecture.
- Baseline B0: p=1/2; a=(9/50,-3/25,2/25); b=(1/10,2/25,-3/50); tau=1/4; epsilon=3/50. Parameters are independent of n and of past truncation M.
- Conditional kernels, exact-event identities and conditional negative association are used for Hermitian strict contractions. Event positivity follows from the margin.
- Numerical linear solves only propose rational dyadic vectors. Exact residuals supply their error bound; all rate-certificate event probabilities are rational and log values are interval-enclosed.
- No exchange of an entropy-rate limit with a derivative is assumed. No novelty or formal proof claim is made.
