# Problem and intended strength

Lyons–Steif Conjecture 9.2 asks for concavity of stationary DPP entropy rate as a functional of a fixed measurable scalar symbol. This route tests fixed even real centres plus odd real multiharmonic affine perturbations, with proof-quality entropy-rate error control.

The exact contract and exclusions are in frozen_theorem_v1.md. Positive finite-window curvature is only a search signal. A certified negative rate gap for one pair is useful elimination evidence, not a proof of the conjecture. No change of the frozen original problem has been made.
