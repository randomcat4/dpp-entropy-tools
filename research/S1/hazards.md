# Mathematical and computational risk ledger

1. Fixed scalar scope: no window-dependent symbol, block-valued substitution or growing circulant limit.
2. Exact events: inclusion determinants alone are not configuration masses; all zero constraints must be included.
3. Fourier orientation: K(i,j)=c_(i-j), past T(r,s)=c_(s-r), past-to-future B(r,j)=c_(-r-j-1). Conjugation conventions must agree.
4. Gauge versus entropy: invariant phases exclude gauge removal but do not prove a positive gap. The two reflected endpoints have the same full law.
5. Infinite past: finite Galerkin convergence needs a uniform spectral margin; a finite all-one conditioning alone is not an infinite-past lower bound.
6. Conditional probabilities: exact-event matrices are indefinite. Their inverse bound is justified by the Hermitian part of JQ, not by falsely claiming Q is positive.
7. Interval direction: prove L<=h<=U; negative-pair certificate is U_endpoint-L_center<0, while a counterexample needs L_endpoint-U_center>0.
8. Rounding: raw high precision is not an error certificate. Residuals, exact divisions, rational probability normalization and outward interval logs are separate checks.
9. Minima: binary entropy is not monotone on all [0,1]; its minimum on a conditioning interval is the minimum of the two endpoint entropies.
10. Limits and derivatives: the finite parity Hessian statement is separate from the entropy-rate value identity. No rate Hessian existence is inferred.
11. Coverage: unique search points, repeated verification runs, probability evaluations and actual invoked work are recorded separately.
12. Independence: phase authors cannot certify their own new lemmas. The final main certificate is frozen before its two existing non-author tasks review it.
