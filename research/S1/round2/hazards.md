# Round-two risk and failure gates

- All three symbols must be fixed and jointly affine in the correlation kernel; natural-parameter or spectral-factor nonlinear paths are not the target chord.
- Non-even is insufficient by itself: a translated even symbol is still in the old gauge orbit. A nonreal cycle invariant must be checked.
- Reflection preserves a DPP law but is not automatically the relation between the two new endpoints. Different means already rule out identical endpoint laws.
- General centre event derivatives need not vanish. Full jets include both -sum p'' log p and -sum(p')²/p, and their individual normalization identities are checked.
- Gauge-translation tangents have zero first event variation even at a non-even centre. Their affine curvature is not the curvature of the nonlinear translation orbit; the orbit acceleration matters.
- Exact-event matrices are indefinite; the probability inverse bound uses the signed accretivity argument, not positive definiteness of the event matrix.
- Finite-range unconditional independence is not a finite Markov property. The original extreme-past residual proof must still be supplied.
- A Fourier L1 feasibility ball is sufficient but not exhaustive. A nonnegative spectral-factor centre can lie outside that ball; a mixed affine direction must nonetheless receive its own safe step from an actual symbol margin.
- Positive acceleration with a large Fisher cost is not a positive Hessian. A small negative eigenvalue is not a rate counterexample proximity metric.
- Independent reviewers must not author the specific frozen proof they audit. Multiple versions of one reviewer are one reviewer.
- First batch maximum 24 centres; windows 6/8. Higher windows require a selected candidate or mechanism, and work must stop when two distinct mechanisms return to the same unresolved sign issue.
- Neither a finite failed scan nor a negative certificate for a fixed pair proves a family theorem or the full conjecture.
