Mathematical target: **INCOMPLETE**. The finite exploration has reached **STOPPED_SUBSTANTIVE** after two distinct bounded units. Certificate and independent audit bindings are supplied in this directory; no positive counterexample or family theorem is claimed.

## What the new unit resolves

The even-centre/odd-direction restriction was removed. The fixed baseline changes its mean, cosine coefficients and sine coefficients, has a nonremovable complex cycle phase, and uses three distinct symbols. Its event scores and Fisher contribution do not vanish. An independent reconstruction checks the baseline jets and feasibility.

The fixed baseline B1 admits a negative true-rate gap enclosure, with outward-rounded summary [-3.034891e-5,-7.346045e-6] nats. The six extreme-past kernels are treated separately for the three symbols and their complements. The strict calculation needs past length four, 288 exact event determinants, interval logarithms and a rational variational residual enclosure. The exact rational endpoints, rather than rounded printed decimals, are the certificate. See [rate proof](rate/proof_or_certificate.md), [certificate](rate/artifacts/r2_rate_n4.json) and [non-author acceptance](review/rate_certificate_audit_report.md).

Conditioning on the other sites reduces the edge to a two-site law with q=|z+tv|²; both its entropy acceleration and Fisher terms are nonpositive. This is a finite, single-edge obstruction. It is not a conclusion for a Fourier direction changing many Toeplitz edges, and no novelty priority is claimed.

## Why the search stops

The phase task's first unit used twelve rational centres with a coefficient L1 margin. The second used six complex spectral-factor centres outside that ball, including four centres reaching a prescribed positive margin at a unit-circle zero. Both units included all mean/cosine/sine directions at windows six and eight. Together with the separate main baseline, the coverage is nineteen centres and thirty-eight full finite Hessians, with no positive direction.

The first unit checks whether the removed symmetry had hidden a direction. Nonzero scores and mixed cosine/sine entries show that it had been removed in practice. The second checks spectral cancellation and small symbol values unavailable in the first feasibility ball. It still has acceleration-positive directions whose Fisher cost is larger, while the reported low-Fisher directions have negative acceleration. These observations describe the reported directions in the tested matrices, not a universal inequality.

Both distinct constructions therefore return to the same missing obligation: a genuinely mixed affine event acceleration must overcome the entire Fisher contribution and then yield a strictly positive fixed three-symbol rate gate. Increasing windows, seeds or centre counts without such a mechanism would not close that obligation. No further phase batch or automatic retry is authorized by this checkpoint.

## Reopening condition

A substantive new mechanism with a checkable sign bound, or an explicit fixed feasible scalar chord with new evidence, can justify reassessment. A positive finite signal still requires (L_-+L_+)/2-U_0>0 with independent bounds for all three symbols. The existing negative fixed-pair certificates and the finite single-edge lemma do not prove or disprove the full Lyons–Steif entropy-rate concavity conjecture.
