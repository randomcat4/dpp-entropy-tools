The event implementation has two independent paths at both the polynomial and point-evaluation levels. For polynomials, `event_poly_signed` forms the signed determinant of the diagonal-shifted matrix, while `event_poly_inclusion` forms the principal-minor inclusion-exclusion sum; the checker rejects any mismatch over the eight masks and also checks normalization (`source-snapshots/pr71_aux/implementation/pr60_aux52_certificate.py:239-279`, `source-snapshots/pr71_aux/implementation/pr60_aux52_certificate.py:587-604`).
