## Reason the Claim 4 gap closes

The earlier C1 Claim 4 gap was not an analytic objection to the idea of a fixed-diagonal radial obstruction; it was that the sign claims in equations (31)-(36) were finite exact certificates and could not be accepted from the author script alone. PR71 now supplies a bound, independently implemented, hash-bound, raw-evidence certificate that reconstructs the event laws and jets from the frozen rational matrices, proves strict legality at the needed kernels, and certifies the three sign outcomes with rational outward logarithm intervals.

That is enough to prove the narrow obstruction: the derivative of `-Hess H(K(s))` in the fixed physical direction `D*` is negative at a legal point where the actual negative Hessian and Jensen checks still have the concave sign. Therefore the fixed-diagonal radial monotonicity sufficient condition is false, while the PR60 Lambda-zero theorem and DPP entropy concavity remain unaffected.
