# Assumption ownership

Main owns the frozen statement; proof contributors cannot silently strengthen or reinterpret its premises. Finite n>=1, fixed coordinate basis, fixed partition into nonempty blocks, Hermitian kernels, natural logarithms. Pinching uses the same partition at both endpoints and all affine combinations. The sign convention is J_t(K0,K1)=H((1-t)K0+tK1)-(1-t)H(K0)-tH(K1). Negative J is a concavity violation. Additional spectral hypotheses must appear explicitly in the frozen theorem, including their role in constants and endpoints.
