# Prior-art audit, 2026-09-07

This is a reusable application/corollary project. No novelty or open-problem resolution is claimed. Search was targeted, not exhaustive; absence of a matching search hit is not evidence of priority.

| Source | Relation and checked scope |
|---|---|
| [Lyons 2014](https://arxiv.org/html/1406.2707), section 2.3, Conjecture 2.6; section 5.1, Conjecture 5.3 | Original semantic reference: finite full-subset entropy concavity and scalar stationary entropy-rate concavity are separate statements. This tool proves neither. The source's historical conjecture labels do not establish present open status. |
| [Lyons–Steif](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0204324), Duke Math. J. 2003 | Stationary DPP background only; its abstract concerns entropy estimates and Bernoullicity. No limit-transfer theorem from that paper is assumed here. |
| [Dierckx–Fannes–Pogorzelska 2007](https://arxiv.org/html/0709.1061), section IV, Propositions 4 and 5 | Same proof machinery: finite gauge-invariant quasi-free spectral entropy and relative entropy expressed on the one-particle space. The displayed relative-entropy formula uses the first argument as numerator; support hypotheses matter. These quantum quantities are not the classical DPP Shannon entropy. |
| [Petz 2002](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0209053), Theorem 1 | Known relative-entropy data processing for finite-dimensional trace-preserving 2-positive maps. Occupation-basis measurement is completely positive and trace preserving. |
| [Olshanski 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10723) | DPP/quasi-free-state bridge, application neighbor. A finite construction must still be explained in the proof, not delegated to the title of this paper. |
| [Hino–Yano 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.11024), Theorem 1 | Adjacent information-geometric embedding of a DPP model into a log-linear exponential family. Not a Shannon-entropy concavity or perturbation certificate. |

Search terms included determinantal point process entropy / mutual information / kernel perturbation and quasi-free relative entropy. The finite-block tool should be judged for explicit hypotheses, error accounting, examples and reproducibility, not as a new fundamental entropy inequality.
