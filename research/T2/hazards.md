# Hazards

- Full-subset Shannon entropy is not tr h(K).
- A common coordinate partition is mandatory for affine pinching identities.
- The DPP law of an affine kernel is not generally a mixture of endpoint laws.
- Track the sign of J, including asymmetric endpoint/midpoint errors.
- Positivity of kernels, support of density operators and logarithm domains require explicit handling.
- Spectral constants must expose dimension and boundary degeneration; finite n is fixed in regularization limits.
- A bound on entropy values does not imply a bound on Hessians.
- Low rank alone does not give an area estimate without a norm estimate.
- Finite-window convergence does not imply entropy-rate convergence; fixed-scale and moving-scale statements are distinct.
- Repeated finite blocks and vanishing coupling are not a new scalar stationary mechanism.
- No complex-to-real entropy transfer is assumed.
- Floating arithmetic probes are not rigorous numerical certificates or universal proofs.
