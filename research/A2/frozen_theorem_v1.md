# Frozen decision scope v1

Frozen by route A2 on 2026-09-08. Proof and verification roles may not change
the definitions, endpoint test, or success criterion below.

## Objects and definitions

E={1,...,n} is finite. For 0<t<t0, K_-(t),K_+(t) are real symmetric
positive contractions. K_0(t)=(K_-(t)+K_+(t))/2. Full-event probabilities and
H, Delta have exactly the definitions in problem.md.

## Intent and interface

Study the moving-frame, varying-data or different-approach scope expressly
left outside PR #11. The physical observation basis is fixed. Public prior
proofs and exact arithmetic tools are allowed. A mere change of representation
of the same kernels is not a new mechanism.

## First milestone

Produce either (i) one explicit low-parameter feasible family, with a proof
that its full endpoint/midpoint path cannot be written directly in the old
fixed-data two-term family (allowing a common scalar reparameterization),
followed by an eventwise sign/remaining-gap analysis; or (ii) a self-contained
new path-reduction or obstruction theorem with precisely quantified scope.
The selected family or theorem will be frozen separately before certification.

## Success standard

Any positive Delta requires exact feasibility and an outward-rounded strict
entropy enclosure. Any asymptotic sign claim requires all events' first
nonzero orders, singular/zero-event handling and a uniform remainder over its
stated parameter domain. An independent context which did not create the
proof must audit the frozen final object.

## Explicit non-claims

This decision scope is not itself a universal sign theorem. No arbitrary
orthogonal entropy invariance, curve-Hessian substitution, extrapolation from
finite searches, or claim of audited novelty is allowed. No stationary
entropy rate or unrelated grouped/path-L route is included.
