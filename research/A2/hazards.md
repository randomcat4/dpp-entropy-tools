# Hazards

1. Inclusion minors are not complete event probabilities.
2. Entropy is tied to physical coordinates and not an arbitrary spectral basis.
3. The center of an outer nonlinear family need not be the chord midpoint.
4. Old strict negativity has no automatically uniform rate near zero direction.
5. Pluecker zeros that open under rotation change the entropy valuation.
6. Singular endpoints cannot be checked using a strict Schur inverse shortcut.
7. Scalar reparameterization and internal frame gauge can fake novelty of paths.
8. Vanishing leading coefficients require eventwise remainder control.
9. Floating positivity and bounded non-hits do not settle the real question.
10. Final audit must be independent of authorship and bound to fixed content.
