# Assumptions and operating scope

- Finite fixed dimension and real symmetric positive-contraction endpoints.
- Exact-event inclusion-exclusion, natural logarithms, 0 log 0=0.
- Fixed physical coordinates; only signed coordinate permutations are freely
  entropy-preserving observation symmetries.
- Parameter-dependent frames, projections or directions are permitted.
- Selection of a concrete family requires its own frozen statement before
  a sign theorem is claimed; authors cannot change its assumptions.
- No uniform asymptotic conclusion follows merely from fixed-data big-O.
- Endpoint and true arithmetic-midpoint feasibility must be checked.
- Floating signs are probes; finite non-hits are not universal conclusions.
- Novelty is unconfirmed and is not the basic delivery criterion.
