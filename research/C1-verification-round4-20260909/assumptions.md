# Assumption checks

- Use every labelled DPP event, natural logarithms and the actual kernel-affine
  parameter. A statistic may test full relative entropy without replacing it.
- Strict finite kernels protect event denominators; boundary entropy values
  require a stated continuity argument. Uniform constants need uniform margins.
- PR53's rate divides an even/odd joining mutual information by its parity
  block size; the original scalar entropy divides by twice that size.
- PR54's stationary rate is per two-site cell. Its matching has n−|d| pairs
  and 2n visible coordinates, so boundary count and normalization matter.
- A strong-Rayleigh/SCP coupling assertion needs its actual conditioning
  hypotheses. Pairwise negative correlation does not replace stochastic covering.
- Fixed-marginal and decoupled-center hypotheses must justify the zero first
  information derivative used by a local-curvature argument.
- Any derivative bound divided into a radius needs a positive constant or an
  explicit zero-case convention. Pointwise entropy limits alone do not justify
  exchanging derivatives and limits.
- Only the original specified files are frozen for first review. Author
  additions remain separately versioned and unreviewed unless reassigned.
