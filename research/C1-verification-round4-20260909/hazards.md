# Review hazards

1. Parity permutation is legitimate; Fourier or spectral basis rotation would
   change observed configurations.
2. Event-matrix inverse norm must hold for every zero/one pattern. A lower
   bound on a few event probabilities is not configuration-uniform control.
3. Inverse decay and convergence do not supply the missing entropy sign.
4. Fermionic quantum entropy and occupation-measurement Shannon entropy are
   distinct; covariance mixing alone does not prove occupation entropy gain.
5. A local strict curvature theorem permits H''(0)=0 and does not prove a
   whole-chord sign. A quartic full-chord deficit also does not imply concavity.
6. SCP must be applied to the law whose moment generating function is bounded,
   with conditional couplings and the exact Hamming Lipschitz constant.
7. Four pairwise covering couplings may lack a common four-way square coupling.
8. Directed stationary flow feasibility, data processing and modified log-Sobolev
   bounds do not supply an unproved trajectory second-derivative estimate.
9. A lower bound with a boundary remainder is not automatically an approximation
   by concave functions with vanishing normalized uniform error.
10. New PR54 addenda are substantive source additions, not an editorial successor
    to which the old three-file verdict transfers automatically.
