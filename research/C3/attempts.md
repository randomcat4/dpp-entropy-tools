**Gauge tangent.** Let D_ij=i(i-j)K_ij and E_ij=(i-j)^2 K_ij. Diagonal
gauge invariance yields p'_S=0 and p''_S(D,D)=p'_S(E) for every exact event.
Hence H''(D,D)=dH(E). This is an exact low-Fisher reduction, independently
reconstructed and checked on small rational objects. A general sign of the
weighted outward derivative is not established.

**Nonadditive cycle shear.** Velocities (1,-1) on harmonics (1,2) have
nonzero triangle flux. Fixed-modulus phase curvature can be positive, even
after the complete Fisher term. The affine tangent also incurs a radial
cost. One fixed feasible candidate C3-M1 had negative full finite curvature;
the complete event data and finite weak-coupling obstruction are in mechanism/.
No added harmonic, full Hessian sweep, or random-seed expansion was run.
The finite weak-coupling remainder is not volume-uniform and is not a rate
theorem. That auxiliary author's proof has its own review status.

**Product replacement.** A different mechanism turns a finite affine ray
through a diagonal kernel into an exact independent-coordinate channel.
Commutation and KL data processing control all mixed retention derivatives,
and finite Jensen inequalities pass directly to entropy rate. The complete
candidate is proof.md with frozen_statement_v2.md. It requires no remainder
estimate, finite bandwidth, or entropy-rate derivative.

**Still open.** General nonradial scalar chords do not lie on a line through

the nonnegative entries of a retention-coordinate Hessian do not control
arbitrary signed directions. A counterexample still needs one fixed legal
triple and a positive independent three-process rate gate. No success
percentage is inferred from a number of tested centres.
