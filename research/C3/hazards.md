# C3 proof hazards

1. Entire configuration entropy, not cardinality or quantum entropy.
2. Retention t must produce B+t(K-B) exactly; sqrt(t) would give a wrong path.
3. The reference product pi may have different marginals from the input.
4. Jeffreys divergence uses both KL directions and contracts only when the
   same channel acts on both arguments. E_i must commute with Q_j.
5. Entrywise Hessian signs imply the uniform positive direction sign, not
   positive semidefiniteness for arbitrary signed directions.
6. Concavity on each half-ray needs the derivative matching argument at zero.
7. Boundary masses require finite entropy continuity; differentiating zero
   probabilities is forbidden.
8. Actual stationary rate follows from three fixed finite Jensen limits,
   not from differentiating h or extrapolating H_n/n.
9. The radial theorem excludes general scalar chords. Its novelty is unknown.
10. All three fixed test symbols and both extreme pasts need independent
    enclosures. Positive gate is (L_-+L_+)/2-U_0, negative gate is
    (U_-+U_+)/2-L_0. Finite negative curvature alone is not a rate exclusion.
