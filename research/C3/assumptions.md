# Assumptions

The radial theorem assumes only finite Hermitian contractions, an interior
diagonal anchor, finite Shannon entropy, and a fixed affine direction. Its
stationary version assumes p in (0,1), bounded measurable real g, and the
actual feasible interval of p+t g. No zero-mean or evenness restriction.

The independent numerical certificate has separate stronger assumptions:
one fixed rational finite-band symbol chord, a positive uniform spectral
margin, exact half-line residual arithmetic, and outward interval logarithms.
These assumptions are never transferred to the general radial proof.

Finite floating diagnostics are exploratory. All Fisher terms are retained.
No independence is assumed in a DPP input; only the replacement coins and
replacement product-reference draws in the proof are independent.
