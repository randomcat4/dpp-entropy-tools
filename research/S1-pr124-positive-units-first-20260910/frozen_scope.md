## Included claims

1. On the equal-strength half-leaf `A=B>0`, for every legal diagonal
   parameter, the occupied and vacant one-sided complete-law forms are
   positive definite; hence the complete Shannon entropy is strictly
   concave along the leaf direction.
2. At a boundary face with one fixed positive normalized edge strength and
   the other strength tending to zero, both one-sided complete-law forms are
   positive definite throughout a punctured neighborhood.  The radius may be
   chosen uniformly when the positive face parameters range over a compact
   subset of the legal region.
3. At each fixed positive weak edge in that neighborhood, positivity persists
   under sufficiently small unequal leaf-diagonal perturbations.  No uniform
   perturbation radius as the weak edge tends to zero is asserted.
4. The residual-only Schur-complement coefficient becomes negative for some
   strong coupling, so that particular residual certificate cannot prove the
   full result there.  This is a method obstruction only.

## Explicit exclusions

- all pointwise-resolvent counterexample claims already reviewed separately;
- entropy-rate claims for infinite DPPs;
- global unequal-strength positivity away from the two certified regimes;
- any uniform local radius reaching the weak-edge face;
- any entropy counterexample inferred from a failed intermediate certificate;
- computational or novelty conclusions.
