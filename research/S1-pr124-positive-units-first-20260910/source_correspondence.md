# PR120 / PR124 source correspondence

## Equal-strength theorem

The equal-strength units in PR120 and PR124 have the same hypotheses and the
same conclusion: `A=B>0`, every legal diagonal parameter, positivity of both
one-sided six-dimensional complete-law forms, and strict concavity of the
complete Shannon entropy along the leaf direction.

They use different certificates:

- PR120 reduces the form into four-dimensional blocks and closes positivity
  through determinant identities and a concavity/end-point argument.
- PR124 decomposes into symmetric and antisymmetric sectors, retains a
  three-node positive chain in the symmetric sector, and proves the remaining
  gap positive by an explicit power-series coefficient argument.

Accordingly, PR124 is an alternative proof of the PR120 equal-strength
statement.  It is not counted as an independent new theorem source, and the
review verdict is established directly for PR124 rather than transferred from
PR120.

## Additional PR124 theorem

PR124 separately treats a punctured small-edge face: one normalized strength
is fixed positive while the other tends to zero.  The Schur complement has one
nonzero limiting eigenvalue and one eigenvalue of order `b^2`; the submitted
asymptotics certify both signs uniformly on compact positive face-parameter
sets.  This unit is not supplied by the PR120 equal-strength theorem.
