# Prior-art and neighboring-work audit

Status: preliminary; absence of a located statement is not an originality
claim.

- [Lyons (ICM 2014)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2707) gives the finite DPP
  inclusion definition, exact generating polynomial, exterior-algebra
  projection law, spectral mixture, and states finite entropy concavity as
  Conjecture 2.6.
- [Lyons--Steif (2003)](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0204324) states the related
  stationary entropy-rate conjecture as Conjecture 9.2.  It is not the finite
  theorem proved here.
- [Hino--Yano (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11024) studies the information
  geometry and embedding curvature of DPP models; its abstract and displayed
  scope do not supply the frozen coefficient theorem.
- The public T1 route certifies a curvature sign only for purely imaginary
  bridge-supported directions at real strict-interior centers.  It also records
  the fixed-face obstruction for two-sided affine paths at a projection.
- The public T2 route certifies a finite block entropy-transfer tool and two
  continuity-boundary examples.  It supplies no real counterexample seed.
- Public T3 PR #6 is a candidate finite certificate tool.  Its seed certifies
  the ordinary positive Jensen gap, not the R2 violation sign; it is not used as
  an authority here.

No located primary source has yet been shown to contain the exact v1
coefficient.  This is `PRIOR_ART_UNCONFIRMED`, not a novelty claim; the result
is currently presented as an elementary structural exclusion tool.
