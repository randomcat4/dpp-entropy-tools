# Problem

For every real symmetric 3 by 3 K with 0<K<I, determine whether the Shannon
entropy of its full DPP configuration is concave in every real symmetric
direction. The exact statement and success gate are frozen in
`frozen_theorem_v1.md`. The inherited scalar reduction is background.

This round's first question is whether exact conditional covariance squares
can supply enough Fisher information at the true trace-constrained optimizer.
The artifacts produce new explicit obstructions, not a global solution.
