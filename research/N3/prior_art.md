# Scope relative to sources

The public baseline and its dispatch document are the evidence baseline.
This round used the assigned inherited n=3 Hessian reduction, exact
conditional covariance identities and previously reviewed boundary results.
It did not repeat the inherited global checks or claim those results anew.

[Hillion and Johnson, A proof of the Shepp-Olkin entropy concavity conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01570)
concerns the entropy of a sum of independent Bernoulli random variables.
That theorem does not directly settle the complete-configuration DPP entropy
target here. Its scope was checked; no theorem from it is invoked in the new
proofs in this round.

Weighted score projection and Sherman-Morrison are elementary background
methods. This round's contribution is the explicit DPP specialization and
checkable obstructions to proposed sufficient inequalities. No exhaustive
prior-art search, independent novelty certification or publication-value
claim is made. Correctness and novelty remain separate assessments.
