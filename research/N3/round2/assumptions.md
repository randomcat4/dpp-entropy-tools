# Fixed assumptions and interfaces

All kernels are real symmetric 3 by 3, with 0<K<I and connected observation
graph. All six symmetric affine directions are retained. Zero graph edges
are allowed if connectivity remains. The full eight-event Fisher form,
the exact rank-one triple-score term, and the complete polynomial Rayleigh
squares are mandatory. No change from natural to affine K parameters may
omit the probability acceleration term.

N>0 and H>0 are inherited established reductions on this domain; their
definitions and the scalar rank-one formula are fixed in the theorem file.
An exact zero means an algebraic identity or a rigorous existence proof,
never a floating-point tolerance. Finite negative searches cannot prove
B0. Every restricted result explicitly states its additional restrictions.
No proof author may modify these assumptions.
