## 1. Exact affine realizability and feasibility

Put u=(1,2,3)/sqrt(14). Then A=(1/100)I+(3/5)uu^T has eigenvalues
(1/100,1/100,61/100). B=S(I-A)S has eigenvalues
(99/100,99/100,39/100). Hence

    (1/100)I <= K(t) <= (99/100)I,  0<=t<=1,

by convexity of both matrix inequalities. Strictness is uniform on this
segment. The entries K_12=3/35 and K_13=9/70 are fixed and nonzero, so the
observation graph is connected even at t=1/2 where K_23=0.
The fixed off-diagonal entries are unequal in magnitude, excluding the
exchangeable family. The interval (L,U) lies in (0,1/8).

This is an affine K parameterization, not a free choice of principal minors.
For every t, define x,y,z from its diagonal and a,b,c from its off-diagonal.
Its actual principal minors obey

    q12=xy-a^2, q13=xz-b^2, q23=yz-c^2,
    r=xyz+2abc-xc^2-yb^2-za^2,
    T=r-xyz+xc^2+yb^2+za^2=2abc,
    T^2=4a^2b^2c^2.

These are identities of polynomials in t, and so are their first and second
derivatives. No division by a,b,c or T is used. Moreover, for the full
polynomial f(z1,z2,z3)=det(diag(z1,z2,z3)+K(t)), direct determinant expansion
gives, whenever {i,j,k}={1,2,3},

    (partial_i f)(partial_j f)-f(partial_i partial_j f)
      =[(z_k+K_kk(t))K_ij(t)-K_ik(t)K_jk(t)]^2.            (2)

Equation (2) is the entire Rayleigh square polynomial, including all t
dependence. Evaluating two conditioning endpoints is not used as a
substitute for that identity. The general principal-minor characterization
in Al Ahmadieh--Vinzant, arXiv:2105.13444, supplies contextual background;
the three-dimensional identity needed here is the displayed direct formula.

## 2. A certificate formula without a square-root sign ambiguity

Let p,J be the eight exact event masses and their six-coordinate Jacobian.
Let s_M=(-1)^(3-|M|), Z=sum 1/p_M, and

    g_j=sum_M s_M J_Mj/p_M = partial_j Lambda.

Then all entries of F_pair=J^T diag(1/p)J-gg^T/Z are rational functions of
K. The only transcendental entries used below are the four logarithms in N.
Write adjN=adj(N) and define

    a_j=tr(adjN E_j),
    T_ij=tr(adjN E_i adjN E_j),
    Htilde=d F_pair+T,
    h=Htilde^(-1)a.

Since N^-1=adjN/d, the frozen H satisfies H=Htilde/d and eta=a/d. Therefore

    beta*sqrt(Z)=g^T h,     d alpha=a^T h.                 (3)

The positive number sqrt(Z) does not affect the sign. This avoids treating
a small numeric beta as zero and allows endpoint signs to be certified
entirely using rational interval arithmetic and rigorous logarithm bounds.

For transparency, the event implementation forms M by subtracting one from
each diagonal entry absent from the event. It uses

    p_M=(-1)^(3-|M|) det M,
    J_M,(ij)=(-1)^(3-|M|)[cof_ij M + 1_(i!=j) cof_ji M].  (4)

Formula (4) follows by expanding the exact configuration generating
determinant and by differentiating its affine entries. It retains the
off-diagonal factor of two, and remains polynomial at zero graph edges.

## 4. Existence conclusion and scope

On the connected strict segment, all event masses are positive and smooth;
N and H are positive definite by the inherited frozen definitions. The
function beta is therefore continuous. The certified opposite signs at L,U
and the intermediate value theorem imply an exact t_* in (L,U) with
beta(K(t_*))=0. Uniform inequality (1) applies to this exact root, so it
satisfies B0. It also applies to any other roots in the same bracket.

No entropy chord counterexample is claimed or sought from this object:
the certified scalar is safely below one. The global beta-zero implication
and possible other root components remain open. Independent nonauthor
verification is required before promoting this author certificate to a
reviewed partial theorem.
