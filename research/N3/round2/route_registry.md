# Round-two route registry

## ROUTE-SQUARE

工作单元: exact full-square jets and the H-optimizing trace direction.
Fastest falsification test / 最快证伪测试: show an actual beta zero or a
Lambda-tangent direction whose acceleration has the forbidden sign.
Outcome: identities and an equivalent optimizer restatement; no new bound.
共同瓶颈: Fisher must dominate the cofactor at a special stationary direction.

## ROUTE-ROOT

工作单元: fixed rational affine kernels, followed by interval certification
only after an actual sign change. Structural fingerprint: topological root
existence on a uniformly strict segment. Outcome: a reachable exact zero
with a uniform scalar value below one; beta is not universally positive.
This is partial existence evidence, not a universal B0 proof.

## ROUTE-WEAK

工作单元: full-score Taylor expansion and a rescaled inverse on a fixed
dense weak-edge ray. Outcome: candidate beta=4 sqrt(s1 s2 s3)+O(t).
This excludes local zeros under fixed interior means and nonzero edge
ratios, with no assertion at degenerate ratios or boundary means.

## ROUTE-LOCK

工作单元: Lambda tangency locks the two conditional odds derivatives;
a Fisher Gram projection adds a concrete nonnegative residual. This is a
new lower bound, distinct from the failed round-one residual deletion.
Its proposed dominance over the cofactor is a stronger sufficient
condition. The final bounded test uses only four existing rational kernels
and at most eight fixed starts each; no expansion of kernel families.
共同瓶颈: an additional positive residual need not suffice for cofactor control.
