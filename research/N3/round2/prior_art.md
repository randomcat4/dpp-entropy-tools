# Sources and scope of the round-two mechanism

The directly used primary source is Al Ahmadieh--Vinzant,
[Characterizing principal minors of symmetric matrices via determinantal multiaffine polynomials](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.13444),
Theorem 3.5 and its three-dimensional discussion. The real symmetric
principal-minor condition involves full polynomial Rayleigh squares. For
the present 3 by 3 kernel, direct determinant expansion proves
Delta_ij det(diag(z)+K)=[(z_k+K_kk)K_ij-K_ik K_jk]^2;
the proofs here need only this displayed identity and its affine jets.
This is a known representation tool, not an entropy inequality.

The second-round coordination source audit also distinguishes the
[Hermitian counterpart](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.05267),
[DPP information geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11024), and
[DPP likelihood geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13486).
They motivate coordinates or compare realizability; no theorem from them
is imported to transfer entropy curvature to affine K coordinates.

Relationship to prior work: the full square identity is a known tool;
local entropy concavity near diagonal kernels was already recorded in
round one. The weak-edge beta limit adds a restricted alignment calculation,
and the interval root is a finite exact object. Neither is claimed to be
an unoccupied theorem or a paper-level solution. Novelty is NOT_CERTIFIED;
the bounded source review does not claim exhaustive prior-art search.
