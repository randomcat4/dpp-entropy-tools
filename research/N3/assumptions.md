# Assumptions

The original target assumes only real symmetric dimension three, 0<K<I,
all real symmetric directions, and exact configuration probabilities with
natural logarithms. Strictness gives positive atoms and smooth entropy.

The N^-1 parametrization is used only at connected kernels, where the
inherited proof establishes N>0. No uniform spectral margin, nonzero-entry
lower bound, exchange symmetry, simultaneous diagonalization, sign-definite
direction or fixed softening rate is assumed for the original target.

Each explicit obstruction has its own stated strict kernel or family.
The projected lower bounds are stronger sufficient targets; refuting them
does not weaken or disprove the original entropy conjecture. Their premises
are never silently substituted for the original frozen premises.
