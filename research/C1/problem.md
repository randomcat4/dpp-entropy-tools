# C1 problem

Task I05-C1-20260909 asks whether exact beta zeros of connected strict real three-point DPP kernels obey det(N) alpha<=1. The six-dimensional optimizer must be the one determined by the full definitions, rather than an arbitrary tangent to Lambda.

The source round proved a safely subcritical exact root and identified failed conditional-projection and locked-odds bounds. This run starts from those results, reconstructs the formulas, and investigates new joint boundary scales and the geometry of exact zero branches. It does not attempt the n>=4 or stationary lines.

The result may be a proof, a certified violating zero and entropy chord, or a partial result with a precise unresolved obligation. Correctness, computational certification, formal coverage and novelty are separate statuses.
