# Assumptions and scope

B0 assumptions are exactly those in frozen_statement.md: connected real symmetric strict contraction of size three, all symmetric K-affine directions, and exact beta=0. Neither a spectral margin nor nonzero individual edges is added to B0.

Any boundary theorem in geometry/ has its own explicit fixed-support, scale, compactness and/or weight assumptions. It is a partial result and cannot replace the B0 quantifier. Numeric parameter tables are finite diagnostics. No uncertain or new asymptotic claim may be used before its review scope is recorded in verification.md.

All logarithms are natural. All event probabilities are positive in the strict domain. Randomness refers only to the eight-atom DPP itself; this run does not use a random search seed.
