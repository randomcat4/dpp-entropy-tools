# C1 hazards

- Exact beta zero, not numeric smallness; root enclosures need feasibility and d alpha on the full bracket.
- The target direction is M^-1 eta/alpha. Arbitrary Lambda tangents do not suffice.
- Preserve all eight probabilities and both derivatives. Off-diagonal bases are Eij+Eji.
- Cov(Tstat) is invertible for every strictly positive law. Jmom and Fpair lose rank at a zero edge. Only M is unconditionally invertible here.
- N positivity at Lambda=0 needs connectedness plus both Rayleigh squares, not continuity alone.
- Asymptotic estimates involving singular M must control rescaled blocks and their inverses. Constants for fixed u do not apply when an entry of u vanishes.
- Exponential L curves identify first derivatives; they do not have K-affine second derivative.
- No uniform safety margin may be inferred from old or new finite roots.
- Pure algebraic realizability, exponential flatness, and a scalar restatement are not Fisher-control theorems.
- Independent formula reconstruction and author arithmetic are not two independent audits of a new main theorem.
